# Routes, connections, and late arrivals

Summer service operates from May 1 through September 30. Kestrel leaves Lantern Quay at 07:10, 09:40, 12:20, 15:10, and 18:00. The crossing to Bracken Island takes 35 minutes. Return departures from Bracken are 08:00, 10:30, 13:10, 16:00, and 18:50. All times in the handbook are lake local time. Boarding closes five minutes before departure, even when the vessel is still tied to the pier.

Minnow's connector leaves Bracken for Mossbank at 08:00, 11:00, 14:00, and 17:00. Its crossing takes 20 minutes; return trips leave Mossbank at 08:30, 11:30, 14:30, and 17:30. The published minimum transfer time at Bracken is 15 minutes. Travelers should not build an itinerary around a shorter connection, even if the piers look close on a map. Crew need time to unload cargo and set the boarding ramp safely.

A passenger on the 07:10 Lantern sailing normally reaches Bracken at 07:45 and can use the 08:00 connector. A passenger on the 09:40 reaches Bracken at 10:15 and can use the 11:00 connector. These are planning examples, not protected reservations. If Kestrel arrives late, Minnow may hold for up to ten minutes only when the operations lead approves and weather permits. There is no automatic hold.

Travelers who miss boarding because they arrived late can move to the next sailing with space without paying a change fee. They do not receive priority over existing bookings. A missed final departure does not create a right to a private charter or overnight accommodation. Weather cancellations use the separate cancellation policy rather than the ordinary late-arrival rule.

From October through April, a reduced timetable replaces these hours. The handbook does not list winter departure times. Ask the booking desk for the seasonal bulletin rather than extrapolating the summer schedule.
