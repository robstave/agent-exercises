# Bicycles, pets, and passenger luggage

Kestrel has six bicycle hooks and Minnow has two bicycle spaces. Bicycle transport costs 2 credits per route crossing, in addition to the passenger fare. Folding bikes in a closed bag count as ordinary luggage if the bag fits under the owner's seat. An unfolded folding bike uses a bicycle space. Membership discounts apply to the passenger ticket, not the bicycle fee. Reserve a bicycle space when booking because an available passenger seat does not imply bike capacity.

Electric bicycles are allowed with the battery fitted securely to the frame. Charging aboard is prohibited. Damaged or visibly swollen batteries are refused. Oversize cargo bicycles and bicycle trailers cannot be carried on Minnow. Crew can refuse a cycle that cannot be secured even if the owner has paid a bicycle fee; in that case the unused bicycle fee is refunded, with passenger travel handled according to whether the traveler still chooses to sail.

Ordinary pets travel free but need a declared booking note. Dogs must remain on a short lead in the marked outdoor area. Cats and other small pets need a secure carrier. Pets cannot occupy seats or block exits. Kestrel allows up to four ordinary pets and Minnow allows two. Those pet limits do not include trained assistance dogs, which stay beside their handlers inside the passenger cabin when needed.

Each passenger may bring one cabin bag and one small personal item without charge. Crew must be able to stow the bag without obstructing the aisle. Larger household moves use the cargo service and its separate packaging rules; buying an extra passenger ticket does not create freight capacity. Hazardous articles remain prohibited in passenger baggage as well as cargo.

At crowded departures, passengers board only when their group is called. Cyclists walk bikes across the pier and wait for crew instructions at the ramp. No passenger should secure a pet lead or bicycle lock to a safety rail. The lake can be rough even on a running service, so riders should pack loose equipment and keep essential medication in their personal item rather than in a cargo tote.
