# Booking records, privacy, and support

The booking desk operates from 06:00 to 19:30 on sailing days. Travelers can reserve in person or through the cooperative's fictional booking portal. This handbook has no live connection to that portal and cannot confirm availability, issue tickets, or change bookings. A request to reserve a seat must go to the desk. Do not invent a confirmation number or imply that discussing an itinerary has created a reservation.

The standard record contains a traveler name, route, sailing time, contact method, and any declared bike, pet, or access requirement. An emergency contact is optional. Northstar does not need a passport number for domestic lake service. Staff check concession eligibility without retaining unnecessary identity copies. Access notes describe assistance, such as ramp use, rather than a medical diagnosis.

Ordinary journey records are deleted 90 days after travel. Financial transaction summaries remain for seven years, with access limited to finance staff. These are different record categories: deleting a journey record does not require erasing legally retained transaction totals. Access plans remain until the passenger withdraws them or they have been inactive for 12 months, whichever comes first.

For a lost booking confirmation, the desk verifies the traveler's name plus their chosen contact method and then sends a replacement privately. Staff never post passenger lists or access notes on the public noticeboard. A family member asking about someone else's travel must provide the traveler's authorization before staff disclose the itinerary. A shared surname is not sufficient proof of authorization.

Complaints go first to Tav Bell, who acknowledges them within two business days. Safety incidents go directly to the duty captain and are not delayed by the complaint queue. A privacy deletion request receives a response within ten business days explaining which records can be removed and which financial records must remain. These response targets refer to desk handling, not the time required for a bank refund to appear.
