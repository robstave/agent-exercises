# Lantern Festival operating bulletin

The fictional Lantern Festival takes place on Bracken Island on August 22, 2026. The usual summer timetable runs during the day. One extra Kestrel departure leaves Bracken for Lantern at 21:30, with boarding closing at 21:25. There is no extra Mossbank connector. Visitors from Mossbank must plan around the final ordinary return connector at 17:00 from Bracken; the late Kestrel does not take them home.

The extra sailing charges the normal Lantern–Bracken passenger fare and accepts ordinary membership and essential-companion concessions. It requires a named reservation and cannot be assumed to have walk-up places. Bicycle spaces remain limited to six. The festival organizer has not purchased a private charter, so tickets are sold through Northstar's usual desk and not through the concert admission vendor.

Lantern decorations are welcome if they use enclosed battery lights. Open flames, sky lanterns, fireworks, and loose fuel containers are prohibited on the pier and aboard. A battery light fitted into a decoration is permitted after a visual safety check, but spare loose lithium batteries remain prohibited. Large decorations that obstruct aisles must be left ashore. Staff will not hold unsafe items aboard for later collection.

The quiet boarding window for passengers who requested sensory support begins at 21:05. It is an earlier boarding opportunity, not a separate sailing or a guarantee of an empty cabin. Travelers needing it should request support at least 24 hours beforehand under the ordinary access policy. Essential companions still need a recorded place. Pet rules and accessible wheelchair positions do not change for the festival.

Weather limits apply to the late sailing exactly as they do during the day. If Northstar cancels it, the unused affected ferry journey is refundable or can be rebooked; festival admission and hotel costs are outside the cooperative's refund policy. The organizer's event cancellation does not by itself mean Northstar has canceled a sailing. A traveler choosing not to attend uses the voluntary cancellation rules unless Northstar also withdraws the service.
