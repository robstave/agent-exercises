# Boarding assistance and accessible travel

Kestrel has a level passenger deck, one accessible toilet, and space for two occupied wheelchairs. Minnow has one occupied-wheelchair position and no onboard toilet. Both vessels accept mobility aids without a fare surcharge. These positions count within total passenger capacity, not in addition to it. Wheelchairs are secured only at designated positions; an apparently empty aisle is not an alternative wheelchair space.

Passengers requesting boarding assistance should tell the booking desk at least 24 hours before departure. Staff can often help with shorter notice, but cannot guarantee trained staff or equipment without confirming the request. An access plan records boarding needs and whether an essential companion is required. It should describe practical assistance, not diagnoses. Passengers do not need to disclose their medical history to the ticket clerk.

The portable ramp at Mossbank works only when the lake gauge reads between 1.2 and 2.6 meters inclusive. Outside that range, occupied-wheelchair boarding at Mossbank is suspended even if the vessel itself is permitted to sail. Lantern and Bracken use floating ramps and do not share this gauge restriction. A low-water access restriction therefore does not mean every passenger service is canceled.

If confirmed accessible boarding becomes unavailable because of the gauge or equipment failure, the affected traveler may take a full refund for unused affected legs or rebook without a fee. The same choice applies to the registered companion traveling with them. Northstar does not require the passenger to accept being carried, transfer out of a wheelchair, or travel separately from an essential companion to preserve a ticket's value.

Crew can guide a person to a seat, operate ramps, and secure mobility devices. They do not provide personal care, medication administration, or lifting from a seat. Assistance dogs travel free and stay with their handler; ordinary pets follow the separate pet policy. Anyone planning a Mossbank trip should check both vessel accessibility and the destination ramp conditions, not just whether a wheelchair place is available.
