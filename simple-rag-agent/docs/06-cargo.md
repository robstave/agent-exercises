# Cargo totes, medicines, and restricted goods

Minnow carries up to six sealed cargo totes on each connector crossing. A standard tote may weigh no more than 18 kilograms and must fit within 60 by 40 by 35 centimeters. The charge is 4 credits per tote per crossing. Membership does not reduce this charge. Kestrel carries ordinary passenger luggage but does not accept unaccompanied freight totes. Businesses sending goods from Lantern must arrange a person to accompany the first leg and hand the tote to the Bracken cargo desk.

Cargo reservations close at 16:00 on the previous business day. Reserved cargo must reach the Bracken desk 30 minutes before the connector departs, earlier than the passenger boarding cutoff. Each tote needs a sender name, recipient name, destination, and contact number. Staff scan it at acceptance and handoff. A booking email is not proof that a tote has physically arrived at the desk.

Clinic medicines travel in a separately reserved insulated tote and have priority over ordinary cargo when weather causes a backlog. The clinic supplies temperature indicators and suitable packaging. Northstar offers no refrigerated storage and does not guarantee a temperature range. A medicine tote remains subject to the same weight and size limits. The priority rule does not authorize overloading or departure in unsafe conditions.

Fuel cans, loose lithium batteries, fireworks, and live animals are prohibited as cargo. A mobility device's installed battery follows the vessel's accessibility safety inspection rather than the loose-battery prohibition. Perishable food is accepted only in sealed, leakproof packaging and remains the sender's responsibility. Staff reject a leaking tote even when it has a reservation.

If Northstar cancels the booked cargo crossing, the sender chooses a refund of the unused cargo charge or the next available cargo slot. Passenger tickets linked to that shipment are assessed separately under passenger cancellation rules. Cargo cannot be left on an unattended pier; if a recipient misses collection, the tote returns to Bracken and the sender must arrange a new handoff.
