# Weather decisions and passenger refunds

The duty captain measures conditions at both the departure pier and the exposed midlake station. A sustained wind of 28 knots or more suspends all passenger sailings. Gusts of 35 knots or more also suspend service, even when the sustained wind is lower. Visibility below 300 meters suspends Kestrel; Minnow requires at least 500 meters because its connector approaches an unlit channel. A vessel-specific restriction does not automatically cancel every other route.

Iona Reed publishes the initial daily operating decision at 06:15. Captains can suspend service later if conditions deteriorate. Forecast improvement alone is insufficient to restart: the duty captain must observe safe conditions for 30 continuous minutes and inspect the destination pier. These thresholds are minimum safeguards, not a promise that sailing will proceed whenever the numerical readings fall below them.

When Northstar cancels a passenger sailing, the traveler chooses either a full refund for the unused affected journey or a no-fee move to another sailing with available space. If cancellation of the first leg makes an unused connecting Northstar leg impossible, both unused legs are refundable. A completed outward crossing is not refunded merely because the return is later canceled. Refunds return to the original payment method within five business days after the request is approved.

If the traveler cancels voluntarily at least 24 hours before departure, the full fare becomes cooperative travel credit valid for 90 days. Later voluntary cancellations receive no credit. This rule differs from an operator cancellation, where a monetary refund is an option. Passengers must not be steered into credit when the operator has canceled their journey.

Northstar does not reimburse hotels, meals, taxis, or bookings with other operators. The Island Access Fund cannot override safety decisions. A clinic passenger stranded by weather should contact the booking desk for available rebooking options, but staff must not promise an emergency crossing or guaranteed accommodation.
