# Tickets, membership, and concessions

A single Lantern–Bracken crossing costs 12 lake credits for adults and 6 for children aged 5 through 15. Children under 5 travel free but still need a place recorded on the booking. The Bracken–Mossbank connector costs 5 credits for adults and 2 for children aged 5 through 15. A return ticket is simply two singles; there is no separate return discount. Payment is accepted by card or prepaid cooperative credit, never cash aboard the vessels.

An annual household membership costs 60 credits and covers at most two adults plus their dependent children living at one address. Members receive a 25 percent discount on passenger fares, rounded to the nearest quarter credit. Membership does not discount cargo charges, special charters, or the membership fee itself. Joining does not reserve seats or allow boarding after the cutoff. A member adult taking both routes pays 9 credits plus 3.75 credits for a one-way journey under the ordinary fare rules.

A registered essential companion travels free when accompanying a passenger whose access plan requires assistance. This benefit is available to members and nonmembers. The passenger's own fare is unchanged. Companion places must be recorded when booking because free passengers still count toward vessel capacity. A companion traveling alone pays the normal fare.

The Island Access Fund can provide one round trip each month for residents traveling to a medical appointment. Applicants contact Tav Bell at least two business days before the trip and provide an appointment confirmation with medical details removed. Membership is not required. Approval covers passenger fares on the necessary routes but does not pay for cargo, meals, or overnight lodging.

Tickets can be transferred to another named traveler before boarding closes, provided concession eligibility is checked again. An adult ticket transferred to a child does not automatically generate a refund; contact the booking desk to recalculate the fare. Lost booking emails can be replaced after identity verification. Staff should never ask a passenger to publish an appointment letter or identity document in a public chat.
