# Northstar Ferry Cooperative: field guide

Everything in this collection is fictional. Northstar is a member-owned passenger and light-cargo ferry service on Lake Orison. It links Lantern Quay, Bracken Island, and Mossbank. It does not operate ocean routes or serve an airport. The cooperative's purpose is to keep island residents connected to healthcare, school, groceries, and mainland employment without turning the lake into a private resort corridor.

The two vessels have deliberately different jobs. Kestrel carries 48 passengers and bicycles on the main Lantern–Bracken route. Minnow carries 18 passengers and up to six sealed cargo totes on the Bracken–Mossbank connector. Neither vessel carries cars. Vehicles needing transport must use a separate operator; Northstar cannot book that service. Passenger capacity includes children but excludes crew, and staff never exceed certified limits to accommodate an event.

Members elect five board representatives each spring. The operations lead, Iona Reed, manages sailing decisions and staff assignments. Membership coordinator Tav Bell handles household records and complaints. The harbor council owns the public piers, so pier construction requests go to the council rather than the cooperative. Boarding support and routine passenger questions remain Northstar's responsibility even at a council-owned pier.

The cooperative runs year-round except during unsafe lake conditions and the annual maintenance closure. Winter service has fewer departures, but fare concessions remain available. Timetables in the route guide are the ordinary summer schedule, not a guarantee that a particular sailing will run. Check the weather decision rules before treating a schedule as confirmed.

These documents describe the fictional 2026 operating handbook. No document specifies actual ticket inventory or a live vessel location. The assistant must distinguish a published policy from a real-time booking confirmation. A question about the next actual departure cannot be answered from this static handbook alone.
